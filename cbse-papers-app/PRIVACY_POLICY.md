# Privacy Policy — CBSE Papers (Previous Year Question Papers)

**Effective date:** 19 August 2026

CBSE Papers ("the app") is built to be used **without collecting any personal data**.

## What the app does NOT do

- ❌ No user accounts or sign-in
- ❌ No collection of names, emails, phone numbers, or device identifiers
- ❌ No analytics, crash-reporting SDKs, advertising, or trackers
- ❌ No location access, contacts, camera, or microphone access
- ❌ No sharing or selling of data (there is nothing to share)

## What the app does

- 📥 Downloads question papers (ZIP/PDF) **only when you tap them** from the official,
  public CBSE websites (`cbse.gov.in`, `cbseacademic.nic.in`). Your device's IP address is
  visible to those government servers, as with any website visit. The app developer receives
  nothing from these requests.
- 💾 Stores downloaded and imported papers **locally on your device** (IndexedDB) so they work
  offline. You can delete them at any time from the **Offline** tab.
- ⭐ Stores favourites/settings locally on your device only.

## Permissions

The Android app requests only `INTERNET` access (needed to download papers). Storage is handled
inside the app's private sandbox; no media/storage permission is used.

## Children

The app is a study utility with no accounts and no data collection; it does not knowingly
collect any information from children under 13 (or anyone else).

## Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be published at this URL with a new
effective date.
